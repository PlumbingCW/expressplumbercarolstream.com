Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Carol Stream, IL

A burst pipe floods a house quickly, and the 24/7 line is open at any hour. Shut off the main water valve first, switch off power in any wet area, open a faucet at the lowest point to drain the line, and then call.

The first five minutes

  1. Find the main water valve, usually in the basement near the meter, and turn it clockwise.
  2. Open a faucet at the lowest level to drain the system.
  3. Switch off the breaker for any wet room and do not touch outlets with wet hands.
  4. Move valuables off the floor and photograph the damage.
  5. Call the 24/7 line and tell the dispatcher where the water is coming from.

If you cannot find the main valve, find it now, not during an emergency. Tag it. Everyone in the household should know where it is.

Why pipes burst in northern Illinois

Water expands when it freezes, and the pressure builds between the ice plug and the closed faucet. The pipe often splits at the weakest point, not where the ice formed. The leak shows up when the ice thaws, which is why a pipe can look fine for hours and then flood.

Pipes in exterior walls, unheated garages, crawl spaces and near vents are the most exposed. A pipe that burst once is often vulnerable again. After repair, ask where insulation, heat tape or rerouting would help.

Frozen but not yet burst

A pipe that has no water coming out may be frozen. Open the faucet so melting water can escape. Warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer, never an open flame. If the pipe is inside a wall, turn up the heat and open cabinet doors.

If you see a bulge or a crack, shut off the water and call. A frozen pipe that has not yet burst is much cheaper to handle than one that has.

Water damage and insurance

After the pipe is repaired, dry the area. Remove wet carpet and padding, open drywall that is soaked, and run fans and dehumidifiers. Mold can start in a day or two in warm, damp spaces.

Homeowners insurance often covers sudden burst-pipe damage but treats gradual leaks differently. Photograph everything before cleanup, keep receipts, and call your insurer early. The plumber's written findings help.

Burst Pipe Repair questions

Do you handle burst pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Carol Stream?

Yes. The 24/7 line takes burst pipe calls at any hour. Shut off the main first, then call.

Where is my main water shutoff?

Usually in the basement near where the water line enters the house, or by the water meter. Find it now and label it.

Will insurance cover a burst pipe?

Many policies cover sudden burst-pipe damage. Check yours and call the insurer early.

Can I thaw a frozen pipe myself?

You can warm it slowly with a hair dryer. Never use an open flame, and shut off the water if you see a crack.

How quickly can a plumber come?

The dispatcher gives you an honest window when you call, and same-day appointments are offered when available.
