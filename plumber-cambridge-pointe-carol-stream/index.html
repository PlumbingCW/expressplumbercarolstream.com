What Cambridge Pointe is

Real estate sites list Cambridge Pointe as a Carol Stream community built by Cambridge Homes, with townhomes, single-family homes and condos or duplexes in one place. The Park District has a Cambridge Park at 760 Woodhill Drive, and Kent Park is at 955 Woodhill Drive. Census geocoding places 760 Woodhill Drive and 1260 Trinity Drive inside the village.

Listing records show addresses on Trinity Drive, Kingsbridge Drive, Brighton Drive and Sandhurst Lane. We found no official boundary, and no source ties the park to the community, so we use these streets as a guide only.

When it was built

One listing site dates the community to roughly 1985 through 1994. That is approximate, and different sections were built at different times. Homes of that age have usually seen their water heaters replaced at least once.

The same site reports 18 floor plans, so houses inside Cambridge Pointe are not all alike. A plumber should confirm what each home has before suggesting a repair.

Who owns which pipe in an attached home

In attached homes, the answer depends on your association documents. A supply line inside your unit is normally yours, while lines serving several units or exterior spigots may belong to the association.

Water heaters, drains and shared walls

Many attached homes keep the water heater in a closet or garage. Know where the shutoff is and what the tank label says. If you smell sewer gas or see a drain back up, stop using water and call.

Water reaches Cambridge Pointe through the same village system as every other Carol Stream address. A tank that is more than ten years old, or one that rumbles when it heats, is worth a look. Water heater replacement and drain cleaning are the usual calls.

A short checklist for owners and renters

  • Learn the unit shutoff and test that it turns.
  • Keep your association contact and emergency number handy.
  • Photograph the water heater label.
  • Tell neighbors and the association quickly about any leak that could cross a wall.
  • Renters should call the landlord first and keep notes of what was reported.

A plumber can still talk through the problem by phone, and a clear description saves time on any visit.

Cambridge Pointe plumbing questions

Where is Cambridge Pointe in Carol Stream?

It is a community inside the village. Listing records show streets such as Trinity Drive and Kingsbridge Drive, and no official boundary is published.

Do I call you or my association first?

For a leak inside your unit, call a plumber. For a shared line or a stain on a common wall, tell the association too.

Are the homes the same age?

No. One listing site gives a range of roughly 1985 to 1994, and sections differ.

Do you service townhomes?

Yes. We discuss the problem by phone first, including which lines the association maintains.

Can renters call?

A renter should contact the landlord first, since repairs usually need the owner's approval.
