Sewer backup: what to do first

A backup is the sewer line telling you it cannot carry the load. Stop using water. Do not flush. Do not run the dishwasher or washing machine. Keep family and pets out of any area where sewage has surfaced. If you have a cleanout, leave it closed unless the plumber tells you otherwise.

After the call, take photos of the affected area before cleanup. Open windows if the smell is strong, and wear gloves and boots if you must move belongings. The plumber will clear the line to restore flow, then run a camera to find out why it failed.

How a sewer line fails

Failures fall into four groups. Roots enter at joints and fill the pipe. A section settles and forms a belly where waste collects. A joint separates or cracks and soil washes in. A collapse closes the pipe altogether. Each one has a different cure.

Homes in Carol Stream span several decades. The village was incorporated in 1959, so laterals from the first neighborhoods may be clay, cast iron or early plastic, and newer developments use plastic. The camera tells you which you have and in what condition, and that is what drives the options.

Cast iron and clay specifics

Cast iron corrodes from the inside, forming scale and eventually thin walls. Clay tile has short sections with many joints, which is where roots enter. Spot repair works if the damage is local. Widespread scale or a joint every few feet points to relining or replacement.

The right choice follows the camera. A line that is round and continuous but rough is a lining candidate. A line with a collapse or a major belly is not. Ask for the footage and a written scope before you sign anything.

Who owns what, and who to call

Generally the homeowner is responsible for the lateral from the house to the connection with the main in the street, though property lines and local ordinances differ. Ask the Village of Carol Stream Public Works at 630-871-6260 where the village's responsibility starts for your address before you spend money on a repair near the curb.

If the backup affects several homes on one street, the village may be able to check the main. The plumber can tell by the camera where the blockage sits relative to the property line.

Sewer Line Repair questions

Do you handle sewer line emergencies at night or on weekends in Carol Stream?

Yes. The 24/7 line takes sewer backup calls at any hour, and a licensed plumber is dispatched the same day when available.

How do I know if the sewer line is broken or just clogged?

A camera inspection answers it. A clog clears with cleaning. A broken line keeps clogging or leaves a visible crack, root mass or belly.

Can a sewer line be repaired without digging up the yard?

Often yes. Lining and bursting reduce digging, though both need the pipe path to suit the method.

How long does a sewer repair take?

Spot repairs can take a day. Lining often takes a day after prep. Full replacement can take several days.

Will my homeowners insurance cover a sewer line?

Many policies exclude sewer line repair and backup unless you added a rider. Check your policy before work starts.
