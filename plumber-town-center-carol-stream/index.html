What Town Center is, and is not

The Town Center is a civic and commercial district, not a residential subdivision. The village describes the Ross Ferraro Town Center at 960 N. Gary Avenue as green space with a gazebo, walking paths, ponds, a visitor center and a large fountain. It was dedicated in 1998 and is named for former mayor Ross Ferraro.

The Daily Herald places it at the corner of Gary Avenue and Lies Road. We found no official boundary for the district, so this page describes the surrounding area loosely and makes no claim about exact edges.

Homes near the green

Fountains at Town Center is a condo and townhome community. A Carol Stream board agenda from August 1, 2005 lists building inspections for it under a letter agreement with Town & Country Homes, and a real estate data site dates the buildings to about 2006. That date is approximate.

Relatively recent condos and townhomes raise the same questions as any attached home. See which lines the association maintains, locate your unit shutoff and keep the water heater label handy.

Water and sewer basics

Lake Michigan water reaches the district through the village system. Buildings with several fixtures and long runs of pipe can take a while to deliver hot water, so tell the plumber how many floors and tenants the problem touches.

For a slow drain, drain cleaning is the usual first step. If water stains a ceiling or wall, leak detection finds the source before anyone opens a wall.

Events and access

The Town Center hosts village events such as the Summer Concert Series, and parking and access can be busy on those days. Tell the dispatcher about any gate, loading area or building code before a visit.

If you manage a building, keep tenant contact details and shutoff locations in one place so a plumber can reach the problem quickly.

Town Center plumbing questions

Is Town Center a neighborhood?

It is a civic and commercial district. Fountains at Town Center is a residential condo community near it.

Where is the Ross Ferraro Town Center?

The village lists it at 960 N. Gary Avenue, at Gary Avenue and Lies Road.

Do you handle commercial plumbing?

Yes. Tell the dispatcher whether you own or rent the space and what is wrong.

Who fixes a shared drain line?

That depends on the building's association or lease. Ask your manager, and call a plumber to find the cause.

Can you work around events at the Town Center?

Tell the dispatcher about access and timing and they plan around it.
