What to do first when the water heater fails

Do three things in order. Turn the heater off at its switch, breaker or gas control. Close the cold-water valve on top of the tank if water is escaping. Open a hot tap upstairs so pressure does not build. These steps protect the floor and the tank while the plumber is on the way.

If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 before anything else. Do not touch a light switch. A tank that is hissing, rumbling loudly or venting steam from the relief valve pipe also needs the heater turned off right away.

Water on the floorShut the cold valve and the power. Do not wait to see if it stops.
No hot waterCheck the breaker or pilot first. If they are fine, call.
Rotten egg smellLeave the house, then call the gas utility.
Rusty hot water onlyOften a corroded tank or spent anode rod. Book a visit soon.

Why a heater stops making hot water

Gas units fail most often at the thermocouple, the gas control valve or the burner assembly. Electric units fail at the heating elements or the upper thermostat. Hybrid heat pump units add a compressor, fan and condensate line to the list.

Each failure has a signature. Lukewarm water that runs out fast on an electric tank points to a lower element. A pilot that will not stay lit points to the thermocouple or a draft problem. Water that is scalding one day and cold the next points to a thermostat. The plumber tests before replacing, because many repairs cost far less than a new tank.

Repair or replace: a fair way to decide

Age, leak location and parts cost decide it. A tank in its first eight years with a bad element is a repair. A tank near or past a dozen years with a seeping seam is almost always a water heater replacement, because the steel itself has failed.

A leak at a pipe connection or the relief valve is a different matter and can usually be fixed in place. A leak from the tank body cannot. Ask the plumber to show you exactly where the water is coming from before you decide.

  • Under 8 years old and leaking at a fitting: repair.
  • Over 10 years old and leaking from the tank: replace.
  • Gas control or thermostat fault on a newer tank: repair.
  • Repeat failures in one year: price a replacement.

Gas, electric and hybrid units

Gas heaters are repaired at the burner, the pilot assembly and the vent. Electric heaters are repaired at the elements and thermostats, which are cheaper parts but need safe power isolation. Hybrid heat pump heaters need airflow, a condensate drain and a clean filter, and a trip code on the display narrows the fault quickly.

A tankless heater is a different machine and is covered on the tankless water heater page. Ask the dispatcher which type you own when you call. The model label on the side of the tank lists the fuel and capacity, and a phone photo of it saves time.

Keeping a tank healthy

Lake Michigan water purchased by the Village of Carol Stream carries chlorine as a disinfectant, and any hard water mineral content settles as scale at the bottom of the tank over time. Flushing the tank once a year removes loose sediment. Checking the anode rod every few years extends tank life, and the leak detection page explains how to confirm a slow drip that is not the heater at all.

A pan under the heater with a drain line, a working relief valve and an expansion tank where the system is closed are the three upgrades that most often prevent repeat failures.

Water Heater Repair questions

Do you handle water heater emergencies at night or on weekends in Carol Stream?

Yes. The 24/7 line takes water heater calls at any hour. Describe the leak or the lack of hot water and the dispatcher arranges a licensed plumber, with same-day appointments when available.

Is it safe to keep using a leaking water heater?

No. Turn off the power or gas and close the cold-water valve. Keep using it only after a plumber has found the source of the water.

How long does a water heater repair take?

Many repairs such as an element, thermostat or relief valve are finished in one visit. Larger repairs depend on whether the part is on the truck.

Can an old water heater be repaired?

Yes, if the tank itself is sound. A failed tank cannot be repaired, and a replacement is the honest answer.

What if my heater is a tankless or hybrid model?

The plumber diagnoses those as well. Give the dispatcher the brand and any error code and mention it is not a standard tank.
