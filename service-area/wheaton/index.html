A county seat with houses from several eras

Wheaton became a village in 1859 and a city in 1890, and the 2020 census counted 53,970 residents in about 20,885 housing units. Homes near Front Street and the downtown grid date from long before the subdivisions on the edges of town. That spread matters for plumbing because the pipe inside the wall depends on when the house was built.

Older houses may still carry galvanized supply lines or clay and cast iron drains. Newer ones often have copper or PEX and PVC. A plumber should tell you which you have before recommending a repair, because a patch that suits one material can fail on another.

Water that comes from Lake Michigan

Wheaton says its water comes from Lake Michigan through the City of Chicago's Jardine plant, with six emergency backup wells held in reserve. Treated lake water is gentler on pipes than raw groundwater, but it still leaves scale where it is heated.

If your water heater is more than ten years old, scale in the tank is a common reason for popping noises and weak hot water. See water heater repair for what a plumber checks first, and water softener and filtration if you want to test your tap for hardness.

Backups and slow drains in mature neighborhoods

Large trees near Cantigny and along older residential streets send roots toward sewer laterals. Roots enter at joints, and the first sign is usually a slow tub or a gurgling toilet. A camera inspection shows the pipe instead of guessing.

Family homes, pools and seasonal use

Wheaton's parks and its Northside Family Aquatic Center draw crowds in summer, and many households run hoses, outdoor faucets and irrigation lines from May to October. An outdoor faucet left connected through a freeze can split the pipe behind the wall, and the leak shows up months later as a damp ceiling.

Shut off and drain exterior lines in the fall. If you find a stain after a cold snap, call early. A small leak found in a week costs far less than a soaked wall found in a month.

Planned work around Wheaton

Not every call is an emergency. Wheaton homeowners also book water heater swaps, bathroom remodels and sump pump upgrades in spring and fall. Scheduling ahead lets you compare options and avoid paying for a rushed visit.

If you are planning a remodel, plumbing installation explains rough-in and inspections. If you want a tank swapped before it fails, water heater replacement covers the steps.
