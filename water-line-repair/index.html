What the service line is

The service line carries water from the village main in the street, through your meter, into the house. It is buried, so failures show up as wet lawn, soft spots, low pressure, a spinning meter or no water at all. Because the pipe is out of sight, the first step is confirming the break is on the line and not inside the house.

Use the meter test on the leak detection page. If the meter moves with everything off and the main valve in the house is closed, the loss is between the meter and the valve.

Who is responsible for what

The Village of Carol Stream buys Lake Michigan water through the City of Chicago, and says in its 2025 water quality report that the village has no lead service lines in its system. It also says a service line material inventory is available from Public Works at 630-871-6260. The boundary between village and homeowner responsibility differs by address, so call before you dig.

A common split is that the village is responsible up to the meter and the homeowner from there to the house. Confirm for your address. A plumber can also tell you what the pipe is made of once it is exposed.

Signs of a failing line

  • Sudden drop in water pressure at every fixture.
  • A wet or sunken spot between the meter and the house.
  • Meter spinning with all taps off.
  • Sound of running water in the basement wall near the entry.
  • Cloudy or discolored water after work on the street.
  • Water bill much higher without a change in use.

Any one of these is worth a call. Several together are urgent.

Low pressure and pressure-reducing valves

If the line is not broken but pressure is low, the cause may be a partially closed valve, a clogged pressure-reducing valve, a corroded line or a municipal pressure change. The plumber measures pressure at a hose bib and compares it to the expected range.

High pressure also does damage. If the pressure is above normal, a pressure-reducing valve protects fixtures and the water heater. See burst pipe repair if a line at the wall is the failure point.

Water Service Line Repair and Replacement questions

Do you handle water line emergencies at night or on weekends in Carol Stream?

Yes. Call the 24/7 line and the dispatcher sends a licensed plumber, same day when available.

How do I know if the leak is outside the house?

Run the meter test. If the meter moves with the main valve closed, the leak is on the service line.

Is my service line lead?

Carol Stream's 2025 water quality report says the village has no lead service lines in its system. Ask Public Works for the inventory for your address.

Who pays for a service line repair?

It depends on where the break is. Ask Public Works before work starts.

Can the line be replaced without tearing up the lawn?

Often yes with trenchless methods, depending on the pipe route and condition.
