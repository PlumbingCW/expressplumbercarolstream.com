Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Carol Stream Express Plumbing collects, how we use it and your choices. Updated November 13, 2026.

Call (630) 822-929024/7 Emergency Line Open

Calls

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we collect your phone number, your address and the details of the plumbing problem so we can dispatch help.

We use this information to arrange service, follow up and improve how we work. We do not sell your personal information.

This website

This site has no forms and does not ask you to enter personal details. Like most sites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and performance.

Embedded maps are provided by Google and are subject to Google's own privacy practices.

Sharing

We share information with the plumbers and service providers who need it to complete your job, and where the law requires it. We do not share it for advertising.

If you ask us to delete information from a call, contact us and we will review the request.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@expressplumbercarolstream.com or raised on a call. See the contact page for our address.

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
