When snaking did not hold

A cable punches a hole through a blockage. It works well for hair and soft debris, and it is fast. It does not remove the grease film or scale that lines the pipe wall, so the line starts to narrow again. When the same drain clogs in weeks, jetting is the next step.

The signs are consistent. The drain runs, then slows. A cable brings back a few strands and the line drains for a short time. Another clog follows. If this pattern describes your line, ask for the camera before the jet so the plumber can see whether the pipe is sound enough for the pressure.

How a jetter works

A jetter pumps water at high pressure through a hose with a nozzle that fires jets forward and backward. The forward jets cut a path, and the backward jets pull the hose along and scour the wall as it returns. The water carries debris to the cleanout or the sewer.

  • Clears grease build-up that cables smear but do not remove.
  • Cuts through light and moderate tree roots.
  • Removes scale and mineral deposits inside older pipe.
  • Restores flow so the pipe can carry its full diameter again.

When jetting is the wrong tool

Jetting is not a repair. If the camera shows a collapsed section, a belly where water pools, or a joint that has separated, high pressure can make a weak pipe worse. The honest answer in that case is to repair or reline the pipe, and the options are on the sewer line repair page.

Plastic and cast iron respond differently. Thin-wall old clay tile needs a conservative nozzle and pressure. Your plumber should tell you which pressure and nozzle will be used and why.

Backups and sewer emergencies: what to do first

If sewage is rising in a tub or a floor drain, treat it as a contamination hazard. Keep children and pets out, do not run water, wear gloves and boots if you must move belongings, and call the 24/7 line. Same-day jetting is available when equipment and a plumber are free, and the dispatcher will say plainly if it is not.

Check the cleanout cap if you know where it is. Do not open it if water is near the lip. A plumber will open it safely and control the flow.

Commercial and multi-unit lines

Restaurants, apartment buildings and small commercial properties often need jetting on a schedule because grease and paper load the line heavily. A recurring visit costs less than an overflow. See commercial plumbing for after-hours service and routine maintenance.

Homeowners with older lines can set a yearly interval after the camera shows how quickly the line refills. Some lines need it once, others every year.

Hydro Jetting questions

Do you handle hydro jetting emergencies at night or on weekends in Carol Stream?

Yes. The 24/7 line takes sewer backup calls at any hour, and the dispatcher arranges a licensed plumber, same day when equipment is free.

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?

It depends on pipe condition. A camera inspection tells the plumber whether the pipe can take the pressure.

How is hydro jetting different from snaking?

A snake clears a path through a clog. A jet scours the wall of the pipe along its whole length.

How often does a line need jetting?

Some lines need it once. Lines with heavy grease or roots may need it annually. The camera shows how fast the line refills.

Will jetting fix tree roots permanently?

No. It cuts roots back, but roots return unless the pipe joint they entered is repaired or lined.
